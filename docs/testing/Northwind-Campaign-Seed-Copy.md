## 1. Email seed — consultation offer

### Subject line options
- Book your complimentary consultation at Northwind Skin Studio
- Not sure where to begin? Start with a consultation.
- Your first step toward a personalized treatment plan

### Preview text
Talk through your goals, ask questions, and get a treatment plan that fits your comfort level.

### Body copy
**Headline:**
Start with a consultation that puts your goals first.

**Body:**
If you’ve been thinking about facials, injectables, microneedling, or a more personalized skincare plan, your best first step is a consultation.

At Northwind Skin Studio, we take a calm, educational approach to treatment planning. That means understanding what you want, answering your questions clearly, and helping you choose the right next step without pressure.

**What to expect:**
- time to talk through your goals
- personalized treatment guidance
- recommendations based on comfort, timing, and skin concerns
- a clear path forward if you’re ready to begin

**CTA:**
Book Your Consultation

### Why this seed matters
- strong fit for Atlas email execution path
- straightforward to approve/edit
- directly tied to the site’s primary CTA

## 2. Email seed — membership promotion

### Subject line options
- A simpler way to stay consistent with your skincare goals
- Explore Northwind’s monthly skin membership
- Want a more structured skincare routine?

### Preview text
For clients who want regular care, member pricing, and a long-term plan.

### Body copy
**Headline:**
Consistent care, built into your month.

**Body:**
Northwind’s monthly membership is designed for clients who want a more structured way to stay on top of skincare, maintenance treatments, and long-term results.

Members receive:
- a monthly treatment credit
- preferred pricing
- priority booking access
- ongoing guidance as your skin goals evolve

If you’ve been meaning to build a more consistent routine, membership can make that process feel simpler and easier to maintain.

**CTA:**
Ask About Membership

### Intentional weakness
This draft should feel useful, but a little generic. Atlas should later be able to sharpen:
- who membership is for
- stronger urgency / value framing
- clearer social proof
